# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-12_

RetroGreat is a browser extension that highlights, on a Moxfield deck page, which
cards have a "retro frame" (1997-border) printing.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** RetroGreat does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal
or usage data. There are no accounts, analytics, trackers, cookies, or ads. No
data ever leaves your browser to us — we operate no servers.

## Network requests

To do its job, the extension makes requests only to two public, third-party APIs,
and only for the deck you are actively viewing:

- **Moxfield** (`api2.moxfield.com`) — to read the card list of the deck on the
  page you have open.
- **Scryfall** (`api.scryfall.com`) — to look up which of those cards have a
  retro-frame printing.

These requests contain only card and deck identifiers needed for that lookup. We
do not control these third parties; their handling of requests is governed by
their own policies:

- Moxfield: <https://www.moxfield.com/help/privacy>
- Scryfall: <https://scryfall.com/docs/privacy-policy>

## Permissions

The extension's host permissions (`www.moxfield.com`, `moxfield.com`,
`api2.moxfield.com`, `api.scryfall.com`) exist solely to read the open deck and
perform the printing lookups described above. They are not used for any other
purpose.
